# Content source policy

The module converts facts into original Foundry VTT data. It does not reproduce
wiki articles, artwork, page layouts, or long passages of source text.

## Source priority

1. The supplied Monster Hunter Monster Manual defines D&D5e statistics,
   challenge ratings, actions, body HP, and saving throw mechanics.
2. The supplied loot-table books define d20 carve/capture tables, material
   slots, quantities, and material-derived abilities.
3. Monsterpedia and game databases define numerical hitzones, elemental
   weaknesses, break thresholds, severable parts, ranks, and quest rewards.
4. MonsterBuddy defines monstie forms, riding data, attack patterns, genes,
   states, and companion information when the monster is present there.
5. Wiki Monster Hunter supplies Spanish names, classifications, ecology,
   appearances, variants, weapon identities, locations, organizations, and
   world context. Mechanical claims are checked against the sources above.

Only actual breakable or severable zones become part-HP pools. Ordinary
hitzones may influence research but are not exposed as detachable parts.

## Weapon conversion

The fourteen principal weapon classes retain their Monster Hunter roles.
Physical attacks are classified as slash, blunt, or shot for hitzone
calculation. Elemental damage is calculated separately and uses the selected
part's fire, water, thunder, ice, or dragon value. Weapon identity also includes
its defining resource or constraint, such as sharpness, ammunition, coatings,
phials, melodies, extracts, shells, or gauges.

## Attribution

Each converted document stores compact provenance flags identifying the source,
conversion version, and whether the result is direct data, an adaptation, or an
original non-magical replacement.
